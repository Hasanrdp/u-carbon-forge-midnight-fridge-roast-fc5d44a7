One line roasting a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight. The fridge doesn't just lock the door — it leaves a pious little note about your own good, taped to the cake it is guarding. That is the whole joke: the midnight rule, the fridge's fake concern, and the evidence of its hypocrisy stuck to the forbidden food itself. The line:

> My fridge locked itself at midnight and left a note saying 'for your own good' — taped to the cake.

It was picked from four fresh candidates written for this bounty and judged pairwise against the judge brief published with the bounty. The bouncer and closing-time angles were dropped — the field is full of them — and this one won for being the tightest (99 characters) with a single turn and a visual punchline at the end. It reads cleanly aloud, targets the midnight refusal itself rather than smart-home grumbling in general, and sounds like something a person would actually say standing at the fridge door.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py && cat out/entry.txt
```

The program is deterministic: it writes the same 101 bytes on every run and verifies them against a pinned SHA-256 and byte count before reporting success. Pure Python standard library, no network, no randomness, no input read.
